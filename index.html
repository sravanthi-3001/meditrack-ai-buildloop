Privacy Notice & Local Data Sovereignty

MediTrack AI operates on a strictly client-first architecture. All health documents, lab biomarkers, medications, and chat history are stored solely in your device's browser localStorage vault. No health data is stored on remote servers. Images and clinical queries are transmitted directly to Google Gemini via encrypted TLS only when you initiate OCR or ask questions. You maintain 100% data sovereignty and can permanently purge all data anytime using the one-click wipe button above.

Multimodal Gemini Vision OCR

Images/PDFs are converted to base64 and streamed to gemini-2.5-flash with strict JSON schemas. Biomarkers, reference ranges, and doctor notes extract in under 1.5 seconds.

2

Human-in-the-Loop Confidence

Every extracted biomarker receives an OCR confidence score. Values below 95% threshold are flagged in amber in the interactive modal for clinician or patient correction before storage.

3

ABDM FHIR R4 Interoperability

Normalized data maps to HL7 FHIR R4 resources: Patient (ABHA number), Observation (LOINC), MedicationRequest (RxNorm), and Bundle (collection) for Ayushman Bharat exchange.
